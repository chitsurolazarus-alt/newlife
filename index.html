About The Church

NEW LIFE EAGLES MINISTRIES INTERNATIONAL is a Christ-centred ministry committed to raising disciples, transforming communities and empowering believers to walk in the fullness of God's purpose for their lives. Through worship, discipleship, evangelism and missions, we seek to impact every nation with the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Meet Our Founder: Apostle Thaddeus Farayi Chinyerere

Born in 1962 in a remote rural area, raised by a single mother as the first son. Life was tough, but God had a plan.

In 1979, while going to address a rally, the Lord spoke in an audible voice: "Thaddeus, Thaddeus, leave everything that you are doing. I want you to go and preach to people." Though he didn't yet recognize the Lord's voice, he obeyed and went to a local pastor who led him to receive Jesus Christ as personal Saviour. He was baptized and from that day, he began preaching through singing — demons fled and the sick were healed.

Married in 1990 to a beautiful wife. They struggled for 6 years to have children, but God blessed them with 5 children.

Served in an organization for 26 years as an Apostle. In 2014, the Lord called him as a missionary, leading to the birth of New Life Eagles Ministries International.

Today, the ministry focuses on planting churches in rural areas, helping orphans, training communities in farming foundations, child evangelism, youth outreaches, and couples meetings. A Skills Training Center is being built to train the disadvantaged with practical skills.

His wife, Dee, is involved in training communities under ANAMED, teaching the use of natural herbs and healthy eating, while also helping in discipleship.

"We pray that God will help us achieve that which He has called us to do — all for God's glory."
